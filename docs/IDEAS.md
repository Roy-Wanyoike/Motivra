# Motivra Ideas Board

Ideas under consideration, ranked by leverage. Status: `considered → specced → scheduled → shipped`. Open a discussion on an idea before turning it into a feature issue. Some of these are deliberately ahead of the roadmap — that is fine; the point is to record the thinking.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Deliberately not on the list

- Customer subscriptions before recurring value is proven (per the build directive).
- Any growth mechanic that could be read as spam (referral abuse, dark patterns).
- AI features that bypass human-confirmed diagnosis or touch the ledger.

Effort key: S < 1 week, M = 1–3 weeks, L > 3 weeks (of coordinated team time, not calendar time).
